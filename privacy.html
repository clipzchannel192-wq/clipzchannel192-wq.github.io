Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The Clipz posting tool is used only by the Clipz team to publish our own videos to our own TikTok account.

What we access

When the Clipz account owner signs in with TikTok, TikTok gives the tool an access token and basic account information (the account's ID, username and display name, and the posting options TikTok allows for it). We use these only to upload and publish Clipz videos to that account and to check that each upload finished.

What we store

The access and refresh tokens are stored only on the Clipz team's own computer. We do not collect, sell or share any data about TikTok users or viewers, and we do not access anyone else's account.

Removing access

The account owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security and permissions → Apps); the stored tokens are then deleted.
